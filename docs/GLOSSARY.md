# Glossary — the family's terms

Private canonical reference. One line per term; the linked repos carry the depth.
Served in the cockpit under the Glossary tab.

## The discipline

- **The pentad** — the five-step life of every claim: **Register** (dated claim +
  criteria before the outcome) → **Seal** (mechanism hash-committed, disclosed only
  at grading) → **Grade** (against public evidence, on a clock) → **Provenance**
  (source + confidence on every row) → **Postmortem** (why right/wrong, fed back).
- **Candidate vs finding** — everything is a candidate until it clears its guards
  (null model, replication, confidence filter); never phrase a candidate as a finding.
- **Pre-registration** — committing claims, criteria, and termination rules BEFORE
  outcomes or trials; the difference between a measurement and a story.
- **Resolution criteria / resolve_by** — how a grader codes hit/miss from public
  evidence, and the date by which they must.
- **Sealed mechanism** — the reasoning behind a claim, hash-committed at claim time;
  each grading is a small reveal.
- **Confidence is earned, never conferred** — high confidence requires verification
  against primary sources; self-declared confidence is capped.
- **Falsifiable consequence** — the concrete observable a model commits to that
  would count AGAINST it; a model that cannot lose is notation.
- **Deletion clause / deletion test** — every model's standing commitment: if its
  consequences fail or stay untested while claims accumulate, it retires to
  notation and the retirement is logged. "Notation, not theory" is the verdict.
- **Termination rule** — pre-registered condition under which a trial's construct
  is declared refuted; honored 6/6 times so far.
- **Documentary-density confound** — the archive-artifact failure mode: a blind
  metric tracking how well events are DOCUMENTED rather than what happened; killed
  two trials; now a mandatory registration guard.
- **Brier score / baseline** — squared-error score of probabilistic claims (lower
  is better), always reported against the model's own naive baseline.
- **Earned hit / lucky hit** — postmortem split: the stated mechanism actually
  operated (earned) vs right for the wrong reason (lucky). The honest hit rate is
  roughly half the naive one.
- **Miss taxonomy** — mechanism-miss / information-miss / calibration-miss /
  criteria-miss: why a claim failed, coded per postmortem.
- **Cross-hit** — attribution finding: model A's claim came true but model B's (or
  a canon) mechanism drove it; the fleet's best steering signal.
- **Graduation / burial** — a hunch's two exits: warmed by scans into a claim or a
  new model, or declared dead and archived.

## The fleet and its kinds

- **Model / sister repo** — one theory, one repo: MODEL.md (premises + falsifiable
  consequences + deletion clause) + ledgers; git history is its version record.
- **Instrument (A–G)** — a model's public identity on the ledger site; the
  letter↔name mapping is itself secret until reveal.
- **Consensus (E) / the freeze** — the ensemble instrument; frozen until the
  October grading so its claims are graded exactly as registered.
- **Model kinds** — FORECASTER (predicts events) · CLASSIFIER/annotator (labels
  claims' structure) · FINDER (audience-model: locates resonant segments) ·
  TRACKER (hunch-tracker: registers intuitions verbatim, scans signals) ·
  GENERATOR (hunch-generator: stochastic intersection draws) · TRACER
  (narrative-model: decomposes narrative competitions) · PROSPECTOR
  (application-model: explores the harness's uses) · TIMER (timing-model: launch
  windows vs attention waves) · ATTRIBUTOR (attribution-model: which mechanism
  did reality use) · MIRROR (a level-1 model of the fleet itself).
- **Level-0 / level-1 / level-2** — the meta ladder: models of the world → a model
  of your models → a model of your revision process. Stop rule: only add a level
  when the ledger below has graded rows for it to explain.
- **Canon / baseline wing** — 15 generally accepted models (supply-demand, Overton
  window, Duverger…) run as CONTROLS: harness validation + the beat-the-textbook
  bar. No deletion clauses — their bad grades are findings, not retirements.
- **Model-seed** — a generator draw hunting a GAP the whole fleet misses; if it
  warms, it graduates into a new sister model.
- **Candidate quarantine** — machine-generated candidates are stored apart from
  operator hunches so the operator's calibration metric never mixes sources.
- **Main instance / template** — one copy of the engine is your MAIN (suites +
  fleet registry, holds no models); models are sibling repos it scaffolds. The
  clean template ships all machinery and no data.
- **Trajectory store** — append-only longitudinal record of every graded claim,
  keyed by model VERSION from git history ("did v2 beat v1" is a query).
- **Learnings ledger** — lessons from postmortems fed back into a model's next
  elicitations.
- **Worldview model** — a model OF a person's model (premises at their confidence,
  extrapolated into domains they haven't addressed) — always labeled "our model of
  their model," never their claims.

## Mechanism vocabulary

- **Installed stakes** — interests a past choice created that now
  force future choices; sides in any contest are found by stakes, not statements.
- **Forcing deadline** — the date by which a converging pressure must resolve;
  gates the dilemma-delay read.
- **E-levels (E0–E14) / the hinge** — the emergence ladder from physics to
  civilization; E8/E9 (minds → coordination) is the hinge; claims are classified
  by the levels they span.
- **Power vs legitimacy / the gap** — who can move an outcome vs who is recognized
  as having the right to; the gap between them is the classifier's core signal.
- **Resonance / structural rhyme** — audience-model: attention follows the match
  between a situation's dynamics and an audience's lived pressure structure —
  never mere topic overlap.
- **Parallel likes** — the other things a segment demonstrably gravitates to; the
  fingerprint by which a segment is found in the wild.
- **Invited stance** — who the content invites the viewer to be (judge, underdog,
  builder, survivor); stance decides which audiences attend.
- **Anti-segment** — topic-matched but dynamics-mismatched audience that will
  bounce; predicted, not discovered by surprise.
- **Weave / omission set** — narrative-model: an event plus causal story, role
  assignment, and the questions the narrative needs the audience not to ask.
- **Vocabulary adoption** — the win metric for narratives: whose TERMS neutral
  outlets use when retelling, not reach or spend.
- **Stage clocks** — seeding → amplification → contestation → consolidation;
  tactics that work at one stage fail at another; post-consolidation reversal
  needs an unabsorbable anomaly.
- **Monitored set / blind spot / arrival dimension** — what dominant observers
  watch gets managed, so resolution tends to arrive from the enumerated
  complement; registrations name it in advance and grading codes the arrival.
- **Wave phases** — pre-release → release-spike → scrutiny-trough →
  consolidation; the trough (audit/backlash discourse peaking as launch noise
  decays) is the window for verification-shaped artifacts.
- **Window × readiness** — GO requires both: a launch into a perfect window with
  an unready artifact is vapor; readiness after the window closes is a museum.
- **Fit conditions A–E** — where the harness transfers: implicit predictions
  exist (A), outcomes resolve (B), no grading loop yet (C), track record
  transfers value (D), and **wrongness is affordable** (E) — being seen wrong
  must be a skill signal, not relational or narrative damage.
- **Recurrence with receipts** — deepening by iterating at system level with
  every pass written to disk; the family's answer to latent-loop reasoning.
- **Key / gate / keying** — emergent-tools: a tool is a key to a gated region;
  keying moves a parameter from given to knob; half-keyed = perturbable but not
  settable.
- **Seal-break** — a tool's output keying downstream practice beyond its
  authorized container; the promotion criterion, and (in the wild) the breakout
  failure mode.
- **Maintenance obligation** — every keyed capability levies a standing bill
  (guardrails, upkeep, narrative maintenance); unpaid bills come due.
- **Attention substrate / budget** — attention above E9 is conserved and
  re-spent; coordination tools compete for it; saturated audiences resonate but
  cannot attend.

## Operations

- **Tiers T1–T4** — probe content (cheap, reversible) → public ledger push →
  template publication → full reveal; tiers launch separately; T4 rides only a
  proven wave.
- **T2a / T2b** — sealed-bets launch ("bets locked, grading dates public, watch us
  get graded" — valid before any grades exist) vs track-record push (after the
  first graded cohort).
- **GO / HOLD / NO-GO / tripwire** — advisory verdicts per tier; a tripwire is the
  pre-agreed condition that flips one (e.g., watch phase = release-spike). The
  advisory advises; the operator decides — overrides are logged, not hidden.
- **Stealth doctrine / validator gate / reveal** — no family names anywhere
  public; every public-bound text passes the fail-closed stealth validator;
  the EM connection is disclosed only at the operator-called reveal.
- **Grading window** — the scheduled period when due claims are graded, reasoning
  unsealed, and the site redeployed (first live window: Oct 2–6).
- **Weekly loop** — the scheduled pass: gather evidence → grade due claims →
  monitors → classifiers → trajectory → postmortems → attribution → one sync.
- **Cockpit** — the local UI (models, tasks, events, assessments, glossary,
  connect) at 127.0.0.1:8787.
- **Inbox bridge** — cockpit instructions appended to ui/inbox.jsonl; a session
  with the watch armed receives each line live ("watch the cockpit inbox").
- **Events queue** — cockpit-submitted url/text/youtube items analyzed by chosen
  models, with assessments written back to the same card.
- **Hunch statuses** — dormant → warming → confirmed (or dead), earned only by
  scans; hunches are stored VERBATIM and amended, never rewritten.
